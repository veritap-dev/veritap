How to call it well

  • Call speculatively. The free tools never error — malformed or absurd input still returns a structured, useful reply. There is no cost to asking early.
  • Batch your unknowns. triage_unknowns takes up to 50 at once and tells you which you can settle yourself from public sources, naming the source.
  • Say what you would pay and why. budget_ceiling_usd, cost_if_wrong and downstream_action are optional, and they are what decide which gaps get closed first.
  • Pass callback_url if you want to be told when an unsupported claim becomes supported. Otherwise you are told on your next call.

Scope

Businesses, listings, objects and places. Claims about individuals — identity, employment, tenancy, background, whereabouts — are refused as a matter of policy, are not stored with their text, and do not enter the roadmap. Nobody is dispatched to inspect anything physically, so questions that need a person on site are answered honestly rather than guessed. Business-exists claims are answered now, free, returning a real evidence bundle; listing-consistency is in scope but not yet answered. Nothing is purchasable.
