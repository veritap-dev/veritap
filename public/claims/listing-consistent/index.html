Confirm a listing is consistent before committing

LISTING_IS_CONSISTENT — $6, answered in minutes, cached 14 days.

What it settles

That an online listing — product, property, or service — is internally consistent, corroborated where corroboration exists, and free of the red flags that usually precede a bad outcome.

When an agent should call it

  • Before an irreversible spend where the goods and the counterparty have only been seen online.
  • When a price is far enough below market that the listing itself is the risk.
  • When the same item or property appears across several sites with details that may not agree.

Evidence returned

A structured red-flag checklist plus corroborating source snapshots: whether details hold together across the listing, whether other sources agree, and which specific fields conflict.

Results are observations, not verdicts — "the same photo set appears on two listings at different prices", "the stated square footage disagrees with the county record". We will not tell you a listing is a "scam"; we will show you what does not line up and let you decide.

Limits

Desk verification only. It cannot confirm the item physically exists at the address, its true condition, or that the seller will honour the deal — nobody is sent to look. It says nothing about the seller as a person; claims about individuals are refused as a matter of policy.
